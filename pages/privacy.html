EGP-2026-001

Registration Privacy Notice

This notice summarizes how information submitted through the EGP Educational Intervention Research Study registration portal will be handled.

Information collected

The registration portal may collect participant contact details, parent or guardian details for participants under 18, session preferences, consent responses, and optional media permissions.

Purpose

Information will be used for study registration, communication, scheduling, administration, participant identification, and the research purposes described in the Participant Information Sheet.

Storage

Submitted information will be sent through a Google Apps Script web application and stored in a Google Sheet controlled by the researcher. Access should be limited to authorized individuals.

Research reporting

Research findings should be reported using Participant IDs or summarized data. Names and direct contact information should not be included in public research reports without separate permission.

Optional media permission

Photography and video permissions are optional. Declining these permissions will not prevent participation in the study.

Questions

Questions may be directed to the researcher using the contact information listed in the Participant Information Sheet.
