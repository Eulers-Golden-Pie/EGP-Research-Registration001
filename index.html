About the session

This educational intervention will introduce participating children to structured learning and memory activities using Euler’s Golden Pie. Participants may complete assessments, take part in guided learning activities, and provide feedback about their experience.

This section currently contains temporary information. A more complete description of the session, activities, goals, and event procedures will be added before registration opens.
