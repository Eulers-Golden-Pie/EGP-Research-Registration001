About the Learning and Memory Intervention

This educational intervention is designed to help children develop learning and memory skills through structured activities using Euler’s Golden Pie (EGP).

The program will take place over approximately three calendar weeks and will include 12 scheduled sessions.

Participants will be divided into two separate groups. One group will attend a morning session, while the other group will attend an evening session. Final group assignments and session timings will be shared with registered parents through the WhatsApp group before the program begins.

Parents are kindly requested to ensure that their child is available for the assigned session each day. Regular attendance is important for both the child’s learning experience and the accuracy of the research findings.

Daily Participation & Prizes

Consistent daily participation is essential. Families who register are expected to ensure that participants attend every scheduled session whenever reasonably possible.

Prizes will be awarded to participants with 100% attendance, as well as to the top winners and runners-up. Complete award and evaluation criteria will be explained during orientation.

Program Schedule

Day 1
Introduction Session

Participants and parents will be introduced to the program, the activities planned for the three-week period, and the overall goals of the educational intervention. Instructions and session expectations will also be explained.

Day 2
Pre-Assessment

Each child will complete a series of assessments before beginning the intervention. These assessments will establish a baseline of learning and memory performance for comparison with the post-assessment.

Days 3–11
Learning and Memory Intervention

Participants will take part in guided learning sessions using Euler’s Golden Pie. Sessions will include structured memory activities, practice exercises, and interactive challenges.

Day 12
Post-Assessment

Each child will complete the post-assessment after finishing the intervention. The pre- and post-assessment results will be compared to evaluate changes in learning and memory performance and support the research analysis.
